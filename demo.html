Example dashboard

Everything on this page is invented. The park, the ranger stations, the alerts and the marketplace listings are synthetic, generated in your browser so you can see what the interface does. No sensor is connected, no request leaves your machine, and no number here means anything.

The real thing — the scripts, their commands and their actual output — is on the main page. It is kept separate on purpose: mixing a demonstration with facts is how a reader stops trusting both.

Tactical map

Simulate a camera-trap detection, then compute a patrol route between the stations. The route deliberately includes randomness: a patrol pattern a poacher can memorise is worse than no pattern.

Sample online-trade leads

One card per suspected listing, in the shape osint_scrape.py emits. Sample data. Every flag is a suspicion for a human analyst to check, never an accusation — and the store name is masked here on purpose, because naming a shop next to the word “crime” is defamation.
